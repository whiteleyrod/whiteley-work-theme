Overview

Models the distal Achilles as a material body with a regional strain field: as the ankle moves into dorsiflexion, deep insertional fibres shorten while superficial fibres lengthen. Adjust shank lean to sweep the ankle angle, use grid exaggeration to make the crowding and spreading of the material grid easier to see, and toggle heel lift or Haglund's morphology to see their effect on the strain distribution.
